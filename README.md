### Key 2026 Season Dates

- **Astronomical Autumn:** September 22, 2026, at **8:04 PM EDT**, marked by the September autumnal equinox.
- **Meteorological Autumn:** September 1, 2026, beginning the meteorological season of September, October, and November.
- **First Day of Fall:** September 22, 2026.
- **End of Daylight Saving Time:** November 1, 2026, at 2:00 AM in locations observing the U.S. daylight-saving schedule.
- **Winter Solstice / astronomical end of autumn:** December 21, 2026.

### What the Equinox Means

The September equinox marks the astronomical beginning of autumn in the Northern Hemisphere. Around the equinox, day and night are approximately equal in length.

Meteorological autumn uses fixed calendar months instead: **September, October, and November**.
